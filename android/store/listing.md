## Full description (max 4000)
Threadlock is a puzzle about timing. Glowing liquid runs through a machine of pipes and junctions. You place the pieces, tap PLAY and watch it flow. The one rule: two colours must never meet in the same junction at the same moment, or they turn to mud.

★ PLACE, PLAY, WATCH
• Drag pieces into the sockets: any piece fits any socket, but only the right one does the job.
• Tap PLAY and watch every drop find its way, one tick at a time.
• Mud, a spill, a cup that stays dry, or a machine that locks up for good: every failure shows you exactly what went wrong. Tap "Show me" and try again.

★ FOUR WORLDS, FOUR LAWS
A city that runs on liquid light, and every district has its own trade, its own look and its own pieces:
• The Lantern Works: separators, turnstiles and tanks keep the colours apart under a great rose window.
• The Apothecary: fill every customer's prescription, measured in doses, with flippers, overflows and tipping buckets.
• The Canal City: canals one barge wide, barges going both ways. Signals, passing places and bridges keep the city moving.
• The Mixing Hall: brew recipes in layers, in the right order, with mixers, twin seals and candles.

★ THREE STARS, THREE WAYS
• Solve: fill every cup.
• Lean: use no more pieces than needed.
• Swift: finish before the clock.
60 levels and 12 challenges, each with a grand machine at the end of its world.

★ THE LAB
Endless fresh machines, easy, medium or hard, whenever you want one more.

★ MADE TO BE WATCHED
• Thousands of glowing drops, bloom and light, each world with its own art and music.
• Every colour plays its own note: a solved machine sounds like a song.
• Hints when you're stuck, never a timer you have to fight.
• Play offline, no wifi needed. Your progress is saved on your phone.

Every piece is a real idea from computer science: race conditions, locks, deadlocks, round robin. You don't need to know any of it to play, but if you do, you'll smile.
